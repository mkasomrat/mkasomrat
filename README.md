## 👋 Hey, look — this is me!

I'm **Muhammad Kawsar Ahmad Somrat** — formerly known as *MKA SOMRAT*, and earlier as *mKaSomraT* / *mKas* — a **Ṭālib-i-ʿilm** (seeker of knowledge), a **Full-Stack Dāʿī**, and a **Philanthropist**.

I'm currently studying **Medical Science (MBBS)** at **Khwaja Yunus Ali Medical College & Hospital (KYAMCH)**, Sirajganj, Bangladesh. Medicine keeps pulling me into the intricate architecture of the human body — but my curiosity doesn't stop there. I'm deeply passionate about **Comparative Religion, Science, Philosophy, Coding & Programming, Artificial Intelligence, Robotics, the Internet of Things (IoT), Literature, Design**, and the many intersections where knowledge, creativity, and human ingenuity converge.

> *"Curiosity → Questions → Learning → Understanding → Growth"*

I believe genuine knowledge begins not with certainty, but with **curiosity** — the courage to question, the humility to learn, and the patience to understand. If you'd like to connect, exchange ideas, collaborate, or just have a meaningful conversation, I'd love to hear from you.
